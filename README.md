# Vendor-Performance-Dashboard

 1. Project Title / Headline

Vendor Performance Analytics Dashboard using SQL, Python, and Power BI**

A data-driven analytics solution designed to evaluate vendor performance, analyze purchasing and sales trends, monitor profit margins, and identify opportunities for improving vendor management.

 2. Short Description / Purpose

The Vendor Performance Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence solution developed to analyze vendor performance using SQL, Python, and Power BI. It processes purchase and sales data to track key performance indicators such as profit margin, purchase contribution, vendor lead time, and vendor profitability.

The dashboard helps businesses identify high-performing vendors, detect vendors generating negative margins, and make data-driven decisions related to vendor selection, purchasing strategies, and cost optimization.

 3. Tech Stack

The project uses the following tools and technologies:

- SQL (MySQL): Used for querying, joining, aggregating, and analyzing purchase and sales records using joins, Common Table Expressions (CTEs), and window functions.
- Python: Used for data analysis and processing with Pandas and NumPy.
- Power BI Desktop: Used to build interactive dashboards, KPI cards, charts, and reports.
- DAX: Used to create calculated measures and business performance metrics.
- Power Query: Used for data cleaning, transformation, and preparation.
- Microsoft Excel: Used for initial data inspection and supporting data analysis.

  4. Dataset Overview

The project analyzes more than 1.2 million purchase and sales records to understand vendor contributions and profitability.

The analysis focuses on information such as:

- Vendor names and purchase quantities
- Purchase and sales values
- Profit margins
- Vendor contribution to total purchases
- Lead times and purchasing patterns
- Vendor-level profitability

5. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard tracks eight key performance indicators:

1. Total Purchase Value
2. Total Sales Value
3. Total Profit
4. Profit Margin
5. Vendor Purchase Contribution
6. Vendor Lead Time
7. Number of Vendors
8. Negative-Margin Vendors

 6. Dashboard Features

- Interactive KPI cards for monitoring business performance.
- Vendor-wise purchase and sales comparisons.
- Profitability analysis to identify vendors with negative margins.
- Vendor contribution analysis to understand purchasing concentration.
- Lead-time analysis to evaluate vendor efficiency.
- Interactive filters and slicers for exploring vendor-level performance.
- Visual reports that support purchasing and vendor-management decisions.

 7. Key Insights and Findings

- The top 10 vendors accounted for approximately 65% of total purchases.
- Approximately 15% of vendors showed negative profit margins.
- Purchasing concentration among a small number of vendors highlighted a potential dependency risk.
- Negative-margin vendors represented an opportunity to review pricing, purchasing costs, and vendor agreements.

 8. Business Recommendations

- Review the pricing and cost structure of vendors with negative margins.
- Evaluate purchasing concentration and assess vendor dependency risks.
- Compare vendor lead times to identify opportunities to improve operational efficiency.
- Use profitability and contribution metrics to support vendor negotiations and purchasing decisions.

 9. Business Impact

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of vendor performance and helps stakeholders identify profitability issues, monitor purchasing patterns, and prioritize vendor-management improvements.

**Project Outcome:** Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard supported by SQL and Python analysis to convert large purchase and sales datasets into actionable business insights.
